Born from clay-toned earth and patient hands

Saajkaari makes Jesmonite decor by hand – every tray, vase and holder is poured, cured, sanded and sealed in small batches, so no two pieces are identical.

Jesmonite is a water-based composite of gypsum and acrylic resin. It is lighter than concrete, free of harsh solvents and takes pigment, marbling and gold leaf beautifully.

We keep waste low, reuse our moulds and design pieces made to stay in your home for years.

Behind the craft

Every Saajkaari piece begins as liquid Jesmonite poured into a handmade mould. It is layered, cured, demoulded, hand-sanded and sealed – a slow process that gives each object its stone-like weight and a finish no two pieces share.
